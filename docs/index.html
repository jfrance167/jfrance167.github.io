Professional Profile

Building secure systems
from the inside out.

I’m from Buffalo, New York, and I’m finishing my cybersecurity degree at Alfred State. I like figuring out how networks work and tracking down the cause when something goes wrong.

In my labs, I’ve analyzed network traffic, mapped vulnerabilities, built security tools in Python, and worked with integrity monitoring. When I’m troubleshooting hardware or writing up a security finding, I want to be able to explain what I found and show how I checked it.

I want my teammates to know they can count on me when there’s a problem. As I build my career, I’d like people just getting into cybersecurity to feel comfortable coming to me for advice.
